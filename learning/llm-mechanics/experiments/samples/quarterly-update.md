# Quarterly update: Q3 2026

Revenue grew to $4,218,350 in Q3, up 12.4% from $3,753,000 in Q2. Gross
margin held at 61.8%, and operating expenses rose 3.1% to $1,902,440.

## Highlights

- New customers: 148 (Q2: 121)
- Churn: 2.3% monthly, down from 2.9%
- Average contract value: $28,503
- Support tickets resolved within 24 hours: 94.6%

## Regional breakdown

| Region | Revenue | Change |
|---|---|---|
| North America | $2,531,010 | +10.2% |
| Europe | $1,180,138 | +15.8% |
| Asia-Pacific | $507,202 | +14.9% |

## Next quarter

We expect revenue between $4.4M and $4.6M, driven by the 37 deals currently
in late-stage negotiation. Hiring will add 6 engineers and 2 account managers
by 2026-12-31.
