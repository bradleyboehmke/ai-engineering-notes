Token rules of thumb

From Lesson 1. Use it for first estimates, and measure anything that matters.

Estimating

English prose1 token ≈ 4 characters ≈ ¾ word · 100 tokens ≈ 75 wordsOpenAI
Words → tokenstokens ≈ words ÷ 0.75 (English only)
CodeAbove the rule: punctuation, indentation, and newlines are tokens
NumbersSplit into chunks (1234567.89 → 123 456 7 . 89)
Non-EnglishOften far above English for the same meaning: up to 15× across languagesPetrov et al.
Whitespace"hello" and " hello" are different tokens

Counting exactly

OpenAI modelstiktoken, e.g. tiktoken.encoding_for_model("gpt-4o") → o200k_basetiktoken
Claude modelsToken-counting API endpoint (returns an estimate; free, rate-limited)Anthropic
Cross-providerCounts differ by tokenizer; another provider's count is only an approximation
Full requestPlain-text counts miss overhead: roles, tools, images, files
